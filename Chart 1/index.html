Tourism jobs are well above 2019, but international visitors are not

Tourism jobs and Australians returning from overseas are above their 2019 levels, while overseas visitor arrivals remain below. Each point compares a quarter with the same quarter in 2019, so 100 means “the same as that quarter in 2019”.

Arrivals are calendar-quarter totals and jobs are quarter-ending estimates. The lines are aligned by quarter, but they measure different things. Source: Australian Bureau of Statistics, Overseas Arrivals and Departures and Tourism Labour Statistics.
